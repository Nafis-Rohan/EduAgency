About Us

Your trusted partner in education

Raj Admission Consultancy Limited

RAC is a leading educational consultant working with more than 100 universities in the UK, USA, AUS & NZ. We have partnered with prestigious institutions including the University for the Creative Arts (UCA), European School of Economics (ESE), Anglia Ruskin University London (ARUL), University of Northampton London (UoNL), Arden University, & Study Group.

Every year we help over 1000 students to achieve their dream of getting a higher degree abroad. Our customer-focused approach is at the heart of our organisational culture over the years through our honesty and integrity.
