Are You a Local Student? You May Already Be Eligible

You qualify as a local student if you are:

01

British Passport Holder

UK citizens with British passports are eligible for government-funded courses.

02

EU Settled/Pre-Settled

EU passport holders with Pre-Settled or Settled Status can access funding.

03

Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR)

Individuals with ILR status qualify for local student benefits.

04

Refugee Status

Those with refugee status in the UK are eligible for government funding.

Local students benefit from government-funded courses and flexible entry routes.

Multiple Ways to Get Into University

No A-Levels or Traditional Qualifications Needed

GCSE Route

Minimum 3 GCSEs at grade C/4+ can open doors to foundation or access courses leading to a full degree.

NVQ Route

NVQ Level 2 or 3 (and similar) can be used as an entry qualification, particularly for vocational degrees.

Work Experience

Relevant work experience supported with employer reference, payslips, or a P60 can count towards your application.

Level 3 Diploma / Qualifi / OTHM

Level 3 Diplomas (including Qualifi, OTHM, and similar) are strong stepping stones into degrees.
