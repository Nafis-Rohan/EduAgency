3 Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us and the services you request, we may collect or use:

  • Identity and contact information: name, postal address, email address, telephone number, date of birth, gender and marital status.
  • Educational and application information: academic qualifications, education and university history, work experience, course choices, application details and information relevant to eligibility for admission or funding.
  • Identification and status information: copies of passports or other photo identification, right-to-work information and, where relevant to the service requested, immigration or settlement-related documentation.
  • Financial information: information such as income, expenditure, payment details or funding-related information where required for the service or application.
  • Account and preference information: registration or account details and marketing preferences.
  • Technical and website information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device information and website usage data, including information collected through cookies where applicable.
  • Communication and service records: emails, telephone or WhatsApp correspondence, records of meetings and decisions, enquiries, compliments, complaints and claims.
  • Recruitment information: contact details, date of birth, employment history, job applications, references, education history, identification documents and right-to-work information where you apply to work with us.

4 Special Category Information

In some circumstances, information provided as part of an application or support request may reveal special category personal data, for example racial or ethnic origin, health or disability information. We only process such information where it is necessary for the relevant purpose and where an additional condition under UK data protection law applies. Where explicit consent is the appropriate condition, we will seek it separately. We do not require special category information unless it is relevant to the service, application or legal requirement.

6 Where We Get Personal Information From

We obtain most personal information directly from you. Where relevant to the service or application, we may also receive information from partner universities or educational establishments, referral partners or authorised agents, employers or referees, funding providers, and other authorised third parties.

7 Who We Share Personal Information With

We may share personal information where necessary with:

  • universities, colleges and other educational establishments you select or authorise us to approach;
  • service providers that support our website, communications, IT, administration or other business operations;
  • funding providers or other relevant organisations where this forms part of the service you request;
  • professional advisers, insurers or auditors where necessary;
  • government bodies, regulators, law-enforcement agencies or other public authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law; and
  • a buyer, successor or relevant adviser in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring or sale of all or part of our business.

We only share information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and require service providers processing personal data on our behalf to protect it appropriately.

8 International Data Transfers

Some universities, service providers or other recipients may be located outside the United Kingdom. Where a restricted international transfer occurs, we will use an appropriate safeguard required by UK data protection law, such as UK adequacy regulations or approved contractual safeguards (including the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to approved standard contractual clauses), as applicable. You may contact us for further information about the safeguards relevant to your personal information.

9 How Long We Keep Personal Information

Our current core retention periods are:

  • Enquiry records: 6 months.
  • Application records: 1 year after completion of the application or related service.
  • Marketing preference/consent records: retained while relevant to the marketing relationship and for as long as needed to record an opt-out or withdrawal.

Other records are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which they were collected, including to meet legal, accounting, regulatory, dispute-resolution or record-keeping requirements. In deciding retention periods, we consider the nature and sensitivity of the information, the purpose of processing, legal requirements and whether the purpose can be achieved in another way.

10 Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised or unlawful access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction. Access is limited to those who need the information for legitimate business purposes. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can, however, be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11 Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

We do not use solely automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects, or similarly significant effects, for individuals in connection with our consultancy services.

12 Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on the circumstances and the lawful basis used, you may have the right to:

  • ask for access to your personal information and related information about how it is used;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information;
  • ask us to erase personal information in certain circumstances;
  • ask us to restrict the use of your personal information in certain circumstances;
  • object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests and direct marketing;
  • receive or transfer certain personal information in a portable format where the right applies;
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

These rights are subject to legal conditions and exemptions. If you make a valid rights request, we will respond without undue delay and normally within one month. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details in section 2.

13 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, understand use and improve services. Where required, we will ask for consent before placing non-essential cookies. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

14 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We encourage you to read the privacy information provided by any third party you visit.

15 Children's Privacy

Our services are generally intended for individuals aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our standard services. If you believe that we have received a child's information in error, please contact us.

16 How to Complain

If you have concerns about how we use your personal information, please contact us first using the details in section 2 so that we can investigate and respond.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you are unhappy with how we have used your personal information.
